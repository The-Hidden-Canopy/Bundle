# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to act professionally, respectfully, and in good
faith. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, malicious disclosure of
private data, and deliberate attempts to weaken the project's safety or
provenance boundaries are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed
to protect contributors, users, or the integrity of the project. Reports
should be made privately through the repository's current maintainer channel.
